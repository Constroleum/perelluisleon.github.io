Buying an original painting: frequently asked questions

Are the paintings originals?

Yes. Every work is a one-of-a-kind original, painted by hand in acrylic on canvas and signed by Pere Lluís León. No copies exist.

How much does shipping cost?

Shipping is charged per order: €30 to Spain, €200 to the EU and the UK, and €400 to the rest of the world.

How do I buy a painting?

Add the work to your basket and send your order. The artist will email you to confirm the details and arrange shipping.

How big are the paintings, and how will one look on my wall?

Each work lists its size in centimetres. In the works catalogue, open a painting to see it at real scale on a wall.

Where is the artist based?

Pere Lluís León paints in Premià de Mar, near Barcelona. You can write to him through the contact page with any question about a work.

Pere Lluís León

Mediterranean expressionist painter. Lives in Premià de Mar and studied at Escola de la Llotja and Massana. Alternates medical illustration work with painting exhibitions.
