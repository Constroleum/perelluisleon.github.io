Career

Trained at the Escola de la Llotja and the Escola Massana in Barcelona, Pere Lluís León has built two careers side by side: a long practice as a medical and scientific illustrator, with thousands of illustrations published in books and specialist journals, and an expressionist body of painting rooted in the Mediterranean, with Cubist influences. He has exhibited in galleries and venues across Catalonia, including Galería Escolà Mar, BCM Galería and CT Art Gallery, and was selected for the Premi Agbar International Art Contest.

Documentation for every work

Every painting is a signed, one-of-a-kind original in acrylic on canvas. On request we send the technical sheet, high-resolution and detail photographs, exact dimensions and information on the work and its series.

Questions from professionals

Do you provide documentation for curators, galleries or appraisers?

Yes. On request we send the artist’s portfolio, high-resolution images and the technical sheet of any work. Write to us from the contact page stating your reason.

Can I see a work in person?

Yes. You can write to the artist to arrange a studio visit in Premià de Mar, near Barcelona.

Are there large-format works?

Yes. Works go up to 195 × 130 cm (Laberint Musical) and the triptych 1-O-17 (3 × 100 × 130 cm).
