Pere Lluís León

La vida en colors

Browse the works

Every work is a one-of-a-kind original, painted in acrylic on canvas by Pere Lluís León in Premià de Mar (Barcelona). From €1,400, shipped worldwide.

Deeply marked by a whole career as a scientific illustrator, Pere Lluís León sets out as a visual artist with a collection of paintings that reflect the themes of Mediterranean life through a liberated, dreamlike lens, brimming with vibrant colours.

Pere Lluís León is a dreamer who delights in the autonomous dance of life. He is driven by the desire to portray our world through an optimistic, almost hedonistic vision, navigating the ebb and flow of human emotions. With a collection titled “La vida en colors”, his work is filled with everyday feelings, embodying inherently human emotions, where the figures within the context align whimsically with the moods of the subject or subjects or, perhaps, boldly defy them.

Framing Pere Lluís León is an arduous task; clearly tinted with Cubist influences, he is a singular artist whose original works spectacularly infuse our Mediterranean lives with colour, distilling extroversion and brimming with symbolism and critique.

Barcelona, 2024.
