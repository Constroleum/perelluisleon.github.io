Medical illustration

Medical illustrations for hospitals, medical schools, medical books and specialist journals, presentations and 2D animation.

A visual communicator specialising in biomedicine, natural sciences and technology. Each illustration combines the craft of drawing with exact scientific and medical knowledge.

Trained in drawing and painting at Llotja and the Escola Massana, he studied medical illustration in collaboration with the Department of Anatomy of the Faculty of Medicine in Barcelona. Since then he has produced anatomy, surgery and microbiology books, and visual communication projects with hospitals and faculties across Spain and Europe.
