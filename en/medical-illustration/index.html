Profile

A visual communicator specialising in biomedicine, natural sciences and technology, combining the craft of drawing with exact scientific and medical knowledge, in both traditional and digital techniques.

After studying fine arts at Llotja and the Escola Massana, he was art director at medical advertising agencies working for pharmaceutical laboratories. He has worked freelance since 1992, and from 1992 to 2005 collaborated with the Department of Anatomy of the Faculty of Medicine in Barcelona, at Bellvitge.

He has produced anatomy, surgery and microbiology books for medical publishers, illustrations for surgical and popular-science journals, and visual communication projects with hospitals and faculties across Spain and Europe.
