The artist

Contemporary expressionist working at the intersection of colour, form, and Mediterranean light. Based in Premià de Mar, Barcelona.

Pere Lluís León Celma

Pere Lluís León lives in Premià de Mar, a small coastal town near Barcelona. From a very young age he showed a clear devotion to the graphic arts. He began studying drawing at a small private academy in Barcelona. His undeniable vocation led him to enrol at the Llotja School to study illustration.

He later continued his training in drawing and painting at the Massana School of Arts in Barcelona. Never losing sight of his future goal, he moved to Paris, Brussels and Rotterdam, where he alternated work with painting studies. On returning to Barcelona, he began working as a medical illustrator for laboratory advertising.

He collaborated with the Bellvitge Faculty of Medicine, creating didactic anatomical illustrations for students’ research work. It was then that he specialised in medical and surgical illustration, also working with the Hospital Clínic, Hospital Esperança, Vall d’Hebron, the UAB, Hospital Universitario 12 de Octubre, the Faculty of Medicine of Barcelona and Hôpital Lapeyronie in Montpellier, as well as with professors, chairholders, surgeons and editors of medical books and specialist journals worldwide.

Over the course of his career, thousands of his illustrations have been published in medical books and scientific journals.

Alternates medical illustration work with painting exhibitions.
